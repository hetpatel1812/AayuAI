Common Questions

Everything you need to know about how Aayu AI works.

Is Aayu AI a substitute for a real doctor?
No. Aayu AI is an educational tool designed to help you understand complex medical terminology. It does not provide medical diagnoses or prescribe treatments. Always consult your healthcare provider.
Is it actually free?
Yes, 100% free forever. This is a student-built project aimed at making health information accessible to every Indian family, without any subscription walls.
What languages do you support?
We currently provide full RAG-powered explanations in English, Hindi (हिंदी), and Gujarati (ગુજરાતી) using state-of-the-art IndicTrans2 models.
Can I upload handwritten reports?
Our OCR works best with typed, computer-generated lab reports (PDFs or clear photos). Handwritten doctor notes or prescriptions may not be parsed accurately.
