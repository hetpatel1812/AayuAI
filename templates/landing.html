Common Questions

Everything you need to know about how Aayu AI works.

Is Aayu AI a substitute for a real doctor?
No. Aayu AI is an educational tool designed to help you understand complex medical terminology. It does not provide medical diagnoses or prescribe treatments. Always consult your healthcare provider.
Is it actually free?
Yes, 100% free. Our mission is to make critical health information accessible and understandable for every family.
What languages do you support?
We currently provide comprehensive health explanations in English, Hindi (हिंदी), and Gujarati (ગુજરાતી).
Can I upload handwritten reports?
Our AI is optimized for printed or typed lab reports (PDFs or clear photos). Handwritten doctor notes or prescriptions are currently not supported.
