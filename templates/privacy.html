Privacy Policy

Last Updated: {{ today_month_year }}

1. Introduction

At Aayu AI, we believe your medical data belongs exclusively to you. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our educational medical analysis tool.

2. Zero Data Retention

When you upload a blood test report, it is processed entirely in memory. Once the analysis is complete and the results are rendered to your screen, the original file and the extracted text are instantly and permanently deleted from our servers.

3. Information We Do Not Collect

  • We do not store your medical reports.
  • We do not share your health data with any third parties, advertisers, or insurance companies.
  • We do not use your personal medical data to train public AI models.

4. Account Data

If you create an account, we store only the basic information required for authentication: your name, email address, preferred language, and encrypted password.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at hello@aayu.ai.

⚕️ Disclaimer: Aayu AI provides educational health insights only. It is not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always consult a qualified healthcare provider.
